# Study on Fish Stock Depletion in Kerala

## Project Title
**A Study on Fish Stock Depletion Which Threatens Livelihoods in the Fisheries Sector in Kerala, with Special Reference to Vembanadu Lake, Kumarakom**

## Project Overview
This dissertation studies fish stock depletion in Kerala and its impact on the livelihoods of people working in the fisheries sector, with particular reference to Vembanadu Lake, Kumarakom.

## Academic Details
- **Degree:** MA Economics
- **University:** Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam
- **Project Type:** Dissertation

## Objectives
- To study fish stock depletion in the study area.
- To understand the impact of declining fish stocks on fishing communities.
- To examine the economic challenges faced by fishing households.
- To understand the importance of sustainable fisheries management.
